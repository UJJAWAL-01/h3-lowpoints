How to read this map

Each hexagon is an H3 cell. A closed depression is a place where the terrain model holds water if nothing drains it. Colour and height show the chosen measure for the depressions inside the cell; cells with no depression are not drawn.

Coarser resolutions are exact roll-ups of the finest one: the deepest depth, and the sum of area and volume.

A depression is a feature of the elevation model, not a flood.
